# Parent fields, official comparison views and source review preparation

This stage compares six page-qualified parent rows with ten independently pinned
GovInfo HTML comparison documents. It preserves literal source observations and
unresolved context before legal review. A URL match, exact substring, complete
rendering equality, parser role or content seal cannot establish a governing
source, historical producer operation, legal meaning, training gold or Lean proof.

The original parent fields retain all 45 physical row fields and their complete
row hashes. The six literal texts contain 20,230 characters. The prior candidate
inventory retains all 280 partition pieces, including gaps, 534 punctuation
observations and 128 unresolved surface references. Producer `subsections_json`
and `raw_json` contain source-credit/note contamination and remain observations.
No prior producer segmentation becomes sentence or statutory gold.

`legal_parent_source_alignment.prepare_parent_source_alignment` takes the FULL
parent text-view report and its independent hash, the closed complete parent
replay bundle, and the complete official document inputs/population hash. It
regenerates the parent rows through the fixed page reader and each official view
through its unchanged source owner. The official inputs have exactly seven
fields: descriptor, raw bytes, independent descriptor/raw hash and byte count,
FULL view report, and independent FULL view-report hash. Missing/refused views
remain explicit, and input population, ordering or stale-pin changes are refused.

The report keeps the FULL parent report once and each FULL official report once.
Every parent/document pair is retained, including pairs with different source
URLs, legal identifiers or editions. Association fields preserve exact producer
declarations; they do not normalize an identifier, coerce an edition, expand a
revision, select a source or filter the comparison population.

Every official normalized-block literal is searched in every available original
parent field. All overlapping matches are retained, including repeated short
blocks. Each alignment refers to the original official block and pairs its
official-v2 selector with a distinct parent-field selector. Equal surface text
does not merge physical occurrences or authenticate historical correspondence.

Each pair has two complete atomic partitions: the original parent field and the
official normalized document view. Every character belongs to exactly one piece
in each partition, with all covering alignment IDs or an explicit uncovered
range. Normalized interblock newline separators remain represented. Geometry and
text hashes refer to complete source texts retained once; long uncovered ranges
do not cause repeated copies of the entire document.

Parent offsets count Unicode code points and UTF8 bytes of the exact decoded
Parquet `text` field. Official-v2 offsets count Unicode code points and UTF8 bytes
of its declared normalized document text. Neither coordinate system supplies
raw HTML byte offsets. Official markup roles such as statute, notes, metadata,
source credit or table cells remain declared parser observations rather than
reviewed legal roles. A normalized block occurrence cannot serve as an original
HTML occurrence or a legacy statutory-paragraph selector.

The additional rendered-field profile is
`all_data_events_strip_each_join_ascii_space/v1`. After complete strict decoding
and markup validation, stdlib HTMLParser data events from head and body are
collected, each event is stripped at its ends, and nonempty fragments are joined
with one ASCII space. Internal whitespace and NBSP are preserved. All event
hashes, lengths and trim observations are retained, along with fragment geometry
and the complete rendered field. This profile executes no browser or CSS.

Every comparison records `exact_rendered_field_equal`. When complete strings are
equal, their current field characters coincide in this separate declared render
origin. That observation does not attest which rendering the historical producer
used, identify raw HTML bytes, convert official-v2 coordinates or authenticate
publisher/source correspondence. Rendered fields, current parent fields and
official normalized views keep distinct identities and coordinate origins.

The retained actual population has all sixty parent/document comparisons and 850
exact normalized-block occurrences. Of those, 230 occur in the six pairs whose
source URLs match exactly; the remaining 620 occur in other documents through
shared metadata or notes. The six URL-matched legal identifiers are 10:7203,
10:7213, 10:7214, 10:7216, 10:7217 and 10:7229. No occurrence is selected.

All six complete parent strings equal their corresponding declared rendered
fields. Whole parent strings and official normalized document strings have zero
exact whole-string matches in either direction. In URL-matched pairs, 208 of 225
official blocks have exact parent occurrences; uncovered ranges include header
data and changed whitespace/NBSP. These observations expose why string matching
alone cannot authenticate governing source or classify every sentence piece.

The actual alignment retains 3,261 atomic partition pieces and 3,628 render data
events. The large 26:141 normalized view contains 184,855 characters and 1,202
blocks, so official and parent bounds are separate. Parent fields are bounded at
65,536 characters each; official normalized views at 256 Ki characters each;
each complete population at 1 Mi characters. Render fields have 256 KiB per-field
and 1 MiB aggregate UTF8 limits, with event count and byte bounds before retention.

Complete nested input reports and replay bundles are preflighted before copying.
The profile bounds documents, rows, blocks, anchors, partition pieces and retained
observation bytes. Limits may only be lowered. The native 16 MiB raw report,
625 KiB compressed payload and 900 KiB whole-result gates remain unchanged.
Over-bound complete reports are refused, with failed attempts preserved; no
truncation, easy-source selection or larger native limit substitutes for custody.

`parent_source_alignment_report_sha256` hashes the FULL report, including its
internal seal. `validate_parent_source_alignment` requires an independent FULL
pin and repeats complete source regeneration. The 54 focused owner cases cover
all-pair overlap, Unicode geometry, both partitions, rendered-field scope,
unavailable sources, external pins, resealed forgeries and pre-retention bounds.
Actual native qualification and independent replay are established separately by
the campaign completion receipt.

The downstream candidate-context inventory preserves every original piece and
surface reference. Intersections reference exact source tables and full selector
identities; the original candidates remain whole, including heading/body mixing.
Definition, authority, antecedent, shared qualifier, temporal origin and referenced
provision slots remain open. The review handoff can identify ten opportunities
from exact markup-declared statute blocks; these are review requests rather than
accepted legal sentences, definitions or autoformalization inputs.

Existing corpus owners already provide bounded content-addressed source versions,
occurrences, interpretation records and proposed graph adapters. Their seals bind
data identity. `corpus_proposed_graph_adapter` preserves independently supplied
AST/semantic declarations and requires the separate statutory source-selector
contract. This stage's markup and parent selectors cannot be inserted into it by
renaming fields or reusing a surface label.

`scoped_definition_resolution` binds complete caller-supplied scope tables and
explicit lookup routes, including unavailable partitions; it does not discover
governing precedence or prove exhaustive semantic resolution.
`legal_source_definition_bridge` supports an explicitly declared, section-local
unary `means` profile. Definition/norm corpus adapters transport bounded declared
profiles and native ASTs while leaving source, context, review and backend flags
false. `means`, `includes`, deeming and normative consequences remain distinct
interpretive obligations. No alignment automatically creates a definition edge.
