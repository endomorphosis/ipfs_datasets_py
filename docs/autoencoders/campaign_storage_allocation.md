# Campaign storage allocation

On 2026-10-15 the campaign storage allocation was authorized to increase from 145,000,000,000 to **160,000,000,000 bytes** (160 decimal GB, about 149.01 GiB). This is a cooperative accounting limit for the exact named roots in the existing reservation ledger. It does not add a disk, resize a filesystem, or grant 160 GB to each worker.

The accounting rule remains: observed apparent bytes in the named roots plus every outstanding full reservation plus the requested reservation must fit the campaign cap. The filesystem must also have enough physically free bytes for outstanding and requested reservations. Retained reservations do not expire because their owner stopped. A cap migration preserves all reservation records, artifact files, root identities, and historical accounting observations. Old receipts keep their original limits and hashes; only the current ledger's top-level limit is migrated under its existing lock.

The distributed training request policy retains its **50,000,000,000-byte per-worker ceiling**. An attempt's own reserved byte limit is unchanged unless that attempt separately requests and obtains a new reservation. CPU, RAM, child-process, scheduler lane, and proof safety policies are independent of the campaign cap.

The generic agent supervisor's **95% physical filesystem usage watermark** is independent of the campaign ledger. Raising this campaign allocation does not clear `host_disk_high_watermark`. Freeing owned disk contents with verified retention and publication evidence, or placing a separately qualified workspace on a larger actual filesystem, addresses physical capacity. The campaign increase does not relax that watermark or replace actual disk telemetry.

Do not edit the cap or ledger while a source-pinned producer is running. The resource owner requires the ledger limit to match its loaded module constant, and producers capture package provenance. A coordinated migration stops and reaps the sole producer, durably accounts for its artifacts, closes only its eligible stopped reservation, preserves a hash-verified ledger backup, checks exact source and ledger preconditions, and applies the source and top-level ledger changes under the original owner and ledger locks. Validate from a fresh process on the canonical workspace tree, record the unchanged reservations and root identities, and resume exactly one producer with a fresh source identity. There is no implicit upgrade or hot-reload contract.

Storage admission is resource permission only. Formalization success criteria remain unchanged: only a successful `lake build <Lib>` provides a Lean admission. Compiler output, decoder output, loss values, resource reservations, DuckDB rows, uploads, and agreement diagnostics do not provide that admission. No Constitution span is thereby formalized or marked `roundtrip_ok`.
