## Remaining work

Independent meaning and applicability decisions remain necessary for selecting
the officer definition and admitting governing uses. Unsigned declaration
custody is distinct from authenticated review and independently justified meaning.
No graph acceptance or training-mask promotion follows from successful replay.

The [norm-profile gap analysis](legal_ir_statutory_norm_profile_gaps_20261015.md)
records why the existing unary bridge cannot encode the two complete use
provisions: approval necessity, alternative effects/direction, actor versus
recipient, capped counts, purpose and pronoun resolution need explicit typed
semantics. The next grammar increment must test those distinctions with positive
fixtures and countermodels while retaining unsupported actual material. Model
training and statutory Lean qualification remain later, separate gates.
