its frozen third request, and the actual storage refusal. The preparation and
its selected 162 cases did not execute. All three attempts failed before a
storage record, native grant, child or output layout was created.

`admission-refusal-v03.json` is a structured transcription of the launcher's
aggregate stderr JSON from the tool result. The first two refusals lacked
numeric observations. `storage-capacity-observation-v01.json` is an earlier
nontransactional read-only sample; it is not evidence of capacity at admission.

`capacity-control-tests.*` records the executed 15-case stdlib synthetic control
suite. `handoff-static-review.json` and `retained-evidence-inventory.json` are
static/metadata audits with their own declared limits. They do not provide new
corpus execution, human review or model accuracy evidence.

The `.py.txt` files retain the exact pending driver and its qualification helper
as reviewable source. The driver also uses the existing repository-owned
`real_source_probe.py` under its original path. Materialization and any retry
must pin the complete reviewed closure and use a new empty attempt through the
existing native launcher. Historical absolute request paths are not portable
instructions or authorization to reuse a claim.

measured physical-space deficit, review boundaries and resumption sequence.
