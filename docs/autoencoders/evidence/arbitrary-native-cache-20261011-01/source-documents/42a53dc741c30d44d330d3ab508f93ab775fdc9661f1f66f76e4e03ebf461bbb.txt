U.S.C. Title 1 - GENERAL PROVISIONS 1 U.S.C. United States Code, 2024 Edition Title 1 - GENERAL PROVISIONS CHAPTER 1 - RULES OF CONSTRUCTION Sec. 2 - "County" as including "parish", and so forth From the U.S. Government Publishing Office, www.gpo.gov §2. "County" as including "parish", and so forth The word "county" includes a parish, or any other equivalent subdivision of a State or Territory of the United States. (July 30, 1947, ch. 388, 61 Stat. 633.)
