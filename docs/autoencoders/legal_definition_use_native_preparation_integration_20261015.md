## Source preparation and review correspondence remain separate

The [sampled-source binding and combined-review integration](legal_definition_use_review_v2_integration_20261015.md)
already records the explicit §101 source-binding replay and its complete
pending population. That single-document source cannot supply a positive
two-document alias component for this preparation profile. Do not manufacture
source identities or applicability to fit the fixture contract. Current native
cases use fictional source declarations and generated fixture credentials,
not actual statutory legal/formal review.

The existing APIs provide a possible narrow composition from sampled-source
binding to a V2 definition review subject. The binding validates the explicit
request, selected source roles, ordered responses and complete population;
`prepare_definition_review_evidence_v2` and
`build_definition_review_subject_v2` separately replay original candidate and
source bytes, every physical context packet and the declared dependency closure.
This increment implements no new public composition between them.

Such a composition must preserve transport-file hashes, full canonical record
hashes and internal producer/report digests in their original namespaces. It
must retain independent input commitments rather than serializing a regenerated
object and presenting it as the original file. The two selected sampler guide
subjects in the retained definition case are not the same population as its
six physical V2 context occurrences. Map exact source/version/view identities,
role intervals and parent contexts while retaining all 560 original subjects;
neither population may silently replace the other.

Source-only packet delivery and blank-handoff migration already exist in their
own domains. They do not establish candidate blindness, authenticated exposure
or agreement with a candidate AST. The signed V2 interpretation subject contains
that AST and has no exposure field. Checked correspondence from unsigned intake
and exposure declarations to authenticated meaning, actual human independence
and downstream gold eligibility remain unresolved requirements.
