## Remaining gaps

The packet's source-grounded nominations need independent meaning and
applicability review before becoming eligible for any signed semantic admission
or training path. An officer definition request would require its own explicit,
independently reviewed interpretation and exact source-role evidence; the
enlisted-member request cannot stand in for it.

The current unary definition compiler does not, by this connection, gain the
assignment/transfer/approval, numeric-cap, purpose or pronoun semantics needed by
the retained norm candidates. Those grammar and interpretation gaps remain
visible. Contextual declarations do not fill them automatically. No training,
checkpoint download, signing, Lean build, publication or production mutation is
performed by the new API.
