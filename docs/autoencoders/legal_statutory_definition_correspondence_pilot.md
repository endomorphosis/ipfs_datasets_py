# Real-source definition correspondence pilot

The pilot reuses the retained 2024 Title 10 source packet for the definition of
`officer` in §101(b)(1), the complete §7213 assignment/transfer paragraph, and the
complete §7214 officer-detail paragraph. All three official page populations
already exist: 560, 26 and 41 source units, respectively. The 627 blank review
guides and eight cross-section attachment nominations are existing preparation,
not new legal reviews.

The edition sources are [§101](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title10/html/USCODE-2024-title10-subtitleA-partI-chap1-sec101.htm),
[§7213](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title10/html/USCODE-2024-title10-subtitleB-partII-chap723-sec7213.htm)
and [§7214](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title10/html/USCODE-2024-title10-subtitleB-partII-chap723-sec7214.htm).

The source-only packet is under
`artifacts/legal-ir-cache-source-qualification-20261015-01/source/` in the parent
workspace. Its exact three acquisition intakes must be reused intact. Identical
HTML bodies retained by older acquisitions do not make their descriptors,
sampling reports or source-version identities interchangeable. The historical
Parquet requests409/410 keep their undeclared editions and original offsets.

## Explicit alternatives for the definition

The same exact definition text can support distinct unreviewed input proposals:

1. An uninterpreted control treats the entire source body as one unary predicate.
   It retains every word but does not resolve the body's internal legal meaning.
2. A structural proposal uses the exact `commissioned` and `warrant officer`
   ranges in a Boolean disjunction. It does not invent a contiguous
   `commissioned officer` range absent from that body. The shared-noun reading,
   adjacent inclusion clause, warrant-officer definition and applicability still
   need independent interpretation.

Neither alternative is selected, accepted, gold or a reviewed meaning target.
An opaque candidate is an engineering control, not permission to flatten the
statute into an opaque gold atom. Any public-owner refusal is retained as a
refusal; no truncated source or forced schema compatibility substitutes for it.

The original V2 compiler and corpus adapter produce each supported candidate.
The existing review preparation creates that candidate's exact occurrence
population and dependency subject. The source-binding owner receives an empty
response list. The correspondence owner then links the exact original source
roles and review population to the unchanged subject and manifest, using
independent original file and canonical commitments. Preserve the two candidate
identities, inputs and link records separately.

## The two uses remain proposals

Retain each complete use paragraph, its official source version, full-block
geometry, literal term mention and open context. `Officers` and `officers` are
plural surface forms. Existing lookup normalization does not turn them into
`officer`; a lexical hypothesis is not an accepted governing binding.

The existing two-definition alias adapter requires another definition candidate.
It cannot represent either actual norm by inventing a consumer definition.
Likewise, a source occurrence must not be presented as a norm assertion just to
create `USES_DEFINITION`. Retain potential connections as pending proposal
records until a supported complete norm interpretation exists.

The full §7213 proposal must preserve assignment/transfer, direction and the
approval condition. Approval must not silently become sufficient as well as
necessary. The full §7214 proposal must preserve the President's role, the
numerical limit, assignment to the Corps of Engineers, assistance purpose and
pronoun reference. A numerical cap needs a reviewed counting frame; it does not
by itself establish a global lifetime total.

The eight existing sampling-context attachments are nominations in their own
schema. A new context packet or unsigned definition correspondence does not
authenticate those nominations or convert a contextual-response domain into an
existing signed meaning-review domain.

## What a successful run establishes

A successful retained run establishes that the public owners can transport and
reconstruct the supported candidate proposals and their source correspondence.
The complete original source populations and negative/control records remain
available. Original subject/manifest commitments stay exact after linkage.

Empty responses remain pending. An unsigned correspondence record authenticates
no reviewer, settles no prose/AST agreement, closes no legal context and grants
no graph, training, evaluation-gold or Lean admission. No signing helper, key,
model inference, training or native compiler is needed for this stage.

The next semantic step is independent source/applicability and legal/formal
review, followed by a versioned complete norm representation and occurrence-level
governing-use decisions. Conditional compilation of an authored fixture remains
separate evidence from the meanings of these actual provisions.
