# Regenerated candidate source-answer histories

`logic/autoformal/corpus_source_history.py` validates a bounded supplied epoch
graph. Genesis delegates to the unchanged candidate application owner; each
revision regenerates its complete current source/typed/adjudication evidence and
authenticates a fixed revision signing domain. It preserves all old groups,
choices, pages, manifests and explicit trust/time premises.

The effect remains copying exact declared source-answer variant references into
candidate snapshots. It supplies no governing-source facts, legal applicability,
reviewed meaning, training gold, accepted graph or Lean admission. Original source
contexts remain `required_unavailable`; seven deferred questions per request remain
pending. Historical activation does not establish current eligibility.

## A flat supplied graph, without an authoritative head

Supply one genesis report and a topologically ordered list of revisions. Every
revision names one exact earlier regenerated predecessor through its FULL
manifest reference. Its generation is the predecessor generation plus one.
Snapshot name, `candidate_source_context_answers` use, complete preparation root,
ordered original request/subject population and question/slot identities remain
fixed. Preparation/source migrations require another profile and are refused.

Two supplied children of one predecessor are retained sibling forks even when
their selected answers agree. All supplied branches must be validly regenerated
or the operation refuses the entire graph. It never qualifies a selected prefix
or discards a conflicting branch. Duplicate aliases, duplicate snapshot roots,
generation gaps, unknown/later predecessors and unsupported descriptors are
refused. No recursive prefix reports are required; immediate predecessor refs and
flat epoch inventories preserve the chain without repeatedly replaying prefixes.

`current_epoch_id` explicitly chooses a supplied leaf. A selected genesis is
allowed only for a genesis-only graph, and supplies no fresh revision authority.
No branch name/generation establishes global uniqueness, a latest version or an
authoritative history. The graph is complete only for the supplied bounded
population; unseen forks and global history completeness remain unverified.
Every visible fork blocks current use under this profile. A later reconciliation
profile must explicitly authorize any fork resolution.

## Explicit historical and current evaluation premises

Each epoch supplies its complete original inputs and independent source,
typed-response, adjudication, signature-group, registry and policy pins. Each has
an explicit `at_time_utc` within its upstream replay inputs. Genesis is regenerated
at its original selected time; each revision is regenerated at its own selected
time. Child evaluations cannot precede their predecessor; no supplied epoch can
postdate the selected current evaluation.

The selected current leaf must regenerate at the outer `at_time_utc`. Old sealed
roots include their original evaluation time. Editing that timestamp or reusing
old eligibility flags cannot make an old report current; compatible current
typed/adjudication evidence must be supplied and regenerated. An old key or
dependency can have a valid historical result while failing fresh current checks.
The timestamp is an explicit caller premise, not independently authenticated wall
time or a legal effective/event clock. `evaluation_clock_authenticated` stays false.

Historical revision actor exclusion uses its own original reviewers plus its
exact ancestor lineage. The parent roots and current body bind that fixed scope.
Adding later descendants or unrelated siblings cannot change an already sealed
historical epoch merely by introducing another original reviewer. Raw historical
roots and activation results must reproduce exactly under the same input/code
premises.

Current projection separately excludes original principal IDs, declared DIDs and
known registered raw public keys from **every supplied epoch**, including other
branches and rejected original claims. An old signing key cannot evade that check
by changing its registry principal/DID label. Unregistered original keys have no
available raw fingerprint; their declared identities remain excluded. These are
declared identity/key checks, not proof of human independence or competence.

## Fresh revision signatures and exact predecessor binding

The fixed new domain reuses original source subjects/dependency manifests and the
shared legal/formal signature engine:

```text
legal-corpus-source-revision-signed-claim/v1
revise_candidate_source_answer_snapshot/v1
legal-autoformalization-candidate-source-history/v1
```

The independently pinned revision registry and policy use the existing closed
trust schemas with this action/audience. Previously authorized adjudicator or
application actors may sign new revision intent under separately selected policy;
old-domain signatures cannot authorize revision. There is no alternate verifier,
default signer/key/registry/clock or caller callback for validation.

`record_source_revision_declaration(request, subject, adjudication_group,
previous_snapshot_group, previous_snapshot_manifest, descriptor, *, epoch_id,
expected_request_sha256, expected_subject_sha256,
expected_adjudication_group_sha256, expected_previous_snapshot_group_sha256,
expected_previous_snapshot_manifest_sha256,
expected_preparation_manifest_sha256, expected_typed_response_manifest_sha256,
expected_adjudication_manifest_sha256, declared_at_utc, rationale, evidence_refs,
limits=None)` authors a sealed unauthenticated candidate.

The descriptor has the same four keys as genesis: `snapshot_id`, `use_scope`,
`generation` and `previous_snapshot_manifest_ref`. Revisions have generation two
or later and an exact non-null `{"record_cid", "sha256", "schema"}` predecessor
ref. Every SHA in authoring is FULL canonical record/root SHA256 including its
CID; payload CID hashes and raw/file hashes are different namespaces.

The body binds the signed epoch ID, exact immediate predecessor manifest/group,
original request/subject/review scope, all current source roots, descriptor, fixed
effect and ordered nine selected variant references. It requires fresh current
adjudication choices; it never carries forward previously active answers without
current revalidation and new signatures. A historically blocked parent may gain
a qualified successor when all current prerequisites genuinely pass; a prior
positive flag alone supplies none of them.

Authoring checks structure and identity, not predecessor/reviewer authority.
`validate_source_revision_declaration(record, *inputs, **pins)` rebuilds the exact
declared body. Complete history assessment independently regenerates predecessors
before using them, so repaired seals or claimed cached flags cannot establish
authority.

Both current legal/formal roles sign the same retained revision bytes. Each claim
references that body in all nine ordered obligations, with exact selected variant
dispositions, `declared_complete` closure and no unresolved entries. Missing or
changed supporting bytes, self references, unresolved answers, wrong roots/epoch/
parent, old-domain claims and partial activation are blocked. The body cannot
precede its predecessor's selected evaluation or current adjudication declaration/
claims, postdate its epoch evaluation, or postdate its own authority issuance.
The unchanged shared engine owns authorization, age, revocation and dependencies.

## Complete replay API and transport

```python
history = assess_source_history(
    genesis_report, genesis_inputs, revision_steps,
    genesis_epoch_id="local:genesis",
    current_epoch_id="local:revision-2",
    at_time_utc=explicit_selected_current_time,
    expected_genesis_manifest_sha256=genesis_full_root_sha256,
    expected_revision_step_pins=independent_revision_step_pins,
)
```

`genesis_inputs` contains the exact named arguments for
`assess_source_applications`: complete adjudication report, application trust and
groups, descriptor, independent pins and closed `adjudication_replay_inputs`.
Optional `limits` lowers that genesis profile. The offered genesis report is
bounded and its full root independently checked before unchanged validation.

Each revision step has exactly `epoch_id`, `adjudication_report`,
`revision_registry`, `revision_policy`, `revision_groups`, `descriptor`,
`expected_adjudication_manifest_sha256`, `expected_revision_registry_sha256`,
`expected_revision_policy_sha256`, `expected_revision_group_pins` and
`adjudication_replay_inputs`, plus optional lower-only `limits`.
The upstream replay object has the unchanged application's closed argument set;
its original source/alignment replay inputs and explicit epoch time are required.
Unknown keys, arbitrary callbacks and custom native value types are refused.

Revision input groups and pins use the unchanged application's closed layouts
with `revision_artifact_id` replacing `app_artifact_id`. Empty groups have null
body IDs; nonempty groups identify one common body. Complete queue ordering and
every independent evidence/observation pin remain required.

One independent step pin has exactly `epoch_id`, `descriptor_sha256`,
`adjudication_manifest_sha256`, `registry_sha256`, `policy_sha256`,
`group_pins_sha256` and `evaluation_time_utc`. These bind metadata and full trust/
source roots, not a single self-hashed replacement for source replay.

Output has five components: `manifest`, `pages`, `records`, `epoch_manifests` and
`epoch_pages`. Each history row embeds one unchanged regenerated epoch group;
epoch wrappers preserve complete original manifests and pages. Top-level refs
bind every wrapper and row, exact parent links, leaves and visible fork parents.
The original snapshots remain independently reconstructible without recursively
duplicating all predecessor outputs in each child.

`source_answer_snapshot_activated_under_selected_profile` inside the raw epoch
group describes that epoch's own selected trust/time. The outer history row's
`current_candidate_refs_eligible_under_selected_history_profile` additionally
requires the explicitly selected fresh revision leaf, its current prerequisites,
the all-history original-actor exclusion and no visible forks. Only then are its
exact nine `current_active_answer_variant_refs` exposed; all other rows expose an
empty list with explicit blockers. Legacy admission flags are retained unchanged;
technical regeneration does not confer legal historical qualification.

## Resource limits and qualification

The initial profile supports up to four epochs, 32,768 total request rows and
512 history pages of up to 64 row refs. Each record/page remains within 1 MiB;
the complete output stays within 64 MiB. Complete native inputs have a separate
512 MiB logical-byte cap, eight million nodes and depth64. Every logical repeated
input occurrence is charged, including shared objects; these are data/work bounds,
not process memory or absolute cryptographic distribution guarantees.

Before any fixed owner starts indexed evidence-body hashing, the entire supplied
native tree is bounded and detached. Cycles, callbacks and unsupported objects
are refused. Every epoch's original-source, adjudication and application/revision
raw plus mixed JSON evidence receives its own unchanged/lowered stage bounds and
an additional **64 MiB whole-history signed-evidence cap**. Known scope, duplicate
name, clock and preparation failures are rejected before expensive replay. Limits
only lower; capacity depends on all interacting caps, so a large four-epoch graph
can be refused even when its epoch count fits. No prefix or branch is cropped.

Genesis validation runs once per operation; each private fixed revision engine
runs complete unchanged adjudication/typed/source validation once. Parent reports
are the already regenerated immediate nodes, never trusted cached eligibility.
Historical code/input migrations and authoritative global head/fork policies are
separate work. `validate_source_history(report, *inputs, **pins)` first bounds the
offered output, repeats all epoch assessment and compares every component/producer
pin. Engineering signed fixtures qualify behavior; actual revision claims,
candidate activations and downstream admissions remain separately counted.
