# Typed statutory hypotheses and reconstruction prerequisites

This integration adopts the existing source-scope, typed relational-norm and
source-to-typed join owners. It advances the representation prerequisite in the
[LegalIR corpus plan](legal_ir_uscode_corpus_improvement_plan_20261015.md).
It does not train a decoder or supply reviewed legal meanings.

The original commits are `e25c119bc`, `886e578d3`, `52e9007ac` and `49bf6d67f`.
They are adopted with their original authorship and explicit provenance.
Eight files from the first commit already existed byte-for-byte on main; only
its statutory correspondence pilot guide was missing. The next three commits
add three owners, three test modules and three guides. All ten newly adopted
files retain their original bodies. The author checkout remains untouched.

## Supported representation

The [scope owner](legal_source_norm_scope_v1.md) binds an unreviewed declaration
to a complete sampled source paragraph and exact character/UTF8 anchors. It
preserves unresolved legal roles and context. A passive grammatical subject
does not establish who performs an action.

The [relational owner](legal_relational_norm_v1.md) accepts a bounded fragment of
the existing typed syntax: one `Object` carrier, explicitly bound variables,
predicates with ordered arguments and declared roles, binary connectives,
negation, quantifiers and the existing O/P/F modal extension. It preserves
implication direction and modal placement through strict native AST roundtrip
and Lean text generation. This performs no actual Lean build.

The [join owner](legal_source_typed_norm_join_v1.md) links every typed structural
path to existing source anchors or an explicit unresolved assumption. It derives
variable binding, argument position, declared role and modal/connective ancestry
from the validated expression. An anchor proves custody of a phrase; its
relationship to a formal node remains a caller hypothesis.

Constants, functions, equality, additional sorts, counting/cardinality, purpose,
anaphora and unrestricted annotations remain unsupported. The source-only
pending route preserves unsupported provisions without manufacturing a typed
target. Full source norm AST, lowered source output, graph and governing-use
results remain absent. All authority flags stay false and gold masks stay zero.

## Parallel work and decoder evaluation

The published sampled-source/primary association and frozen-runtime protocol
remain included from main. A separate, uncommitted
`legal_norm_scope_correspondence_v1` in the canonical checkout accounts for both
source and typed populations under a different request/profile. Preserve that
work and its original qualification. This integration neither imports its
mutable files nor silently treats its receipts as this join's receipts.
Reconcile the boundaries after publication rather than create another owner.

Reuse the existing original-text roundtrip scorer and source-only formula
metrics where their schemas match. A seven-facet formula score cannot preserve
this richer typed arity, quantifiers, binders and modal placement by flattening
them into strings. Keep exact original UTF8 and normalized text, exact typed AST
and graph, fixed-view CE and embedding MSE/cosine as separate outcomes.

The next training input is a reviewed definition connected to an actual norm and
a second scope-valid use. Record dependencies, applicability, disagreements,
prior exposure and untouched evaluation components before fitting. Withhold
targets, teacher labels and target/loss feature caches from ordinary inference;
name any oracle route explicitly. Compare correct, zero and permuted latent
inputs under matched source/context/budget conditions, and report source,
retrieval and exact-surface channels separately.

Preserve existing checkpoints and embeddings. LegalIR, CodebaseIR, IntentIR,
SecurityIR and UI/UX IR keep separate family/schema/version/task/dimension
identities. These symbolic owners do not establish a new 8D, 384D or 768D model,
ModelManager import, Hub release, qualified teacher or distillation result.
