The original Domain losses explain why our acceptance gate needs improvement. The retained Security checkpoint reports fitting token cross entropy 0.05314 and selected validation cross entropy 0.05378; Intent reports 0.09343 and 0.09900. Those are average teacher-forced lexical-JSON token losses. Free generation still gets almost all complete IRs wrong. Over half of the original target-token streams are punctuation; repeated structural tokens can dominate that average. This is evidence to investigate decision-field conditioning and objective weighting, not proof of a single causal training bug. Security makes operator errors in 13 of 16 rows and type errors in 7; Intent makes modality errors in 10, action errors in 4 and object errors in 6. Those are substantive field errors rather than wrapper/schema mismatches.

Embedding reconstruction also needs a reference baseline. Legal fitting MSE is 0.00261116 versus a zero-vector baseline of 0.00260417; tuning MSE is 0.00264774 versus 0.00260417. Its predictions are not near-zero: mean norms are about 1.36/1.34 compared with gold norms near 1, with positive mean cosines about 0.687/0.680. Security and Intent beat the zero baseline on all three splits, yet their exact IR results remain poor. Report MSE, zero/mean-vector baselines, norm/cosine and semantic generation separately. Never use a small raw MSE or average token loss as the sole distillation gate.
