## What the sequence does

1. Typed responses preserve each reviewer's answers, explicit unknowns, evidence
   references and original request identity. Differing answers and disagreements
   remain visible; agreement does not select a governing answer.
2. Separately authorized legal and formal roles can sign an explicit adjudication
   declaration selecting exact existing answer variants or leaving them unresolved.
   Original reviewer identities and known keys remain excluded from adjudicators.
3. A signed genesis application can copy all nine declared source/context answer
   references atomically into a candidate snapshot. A blocked request activates
   none; seven semantic/proof questions remain deferred.
4. History replays complete supplied epochs and exact predecessors. Visible forks
   remain blocking; a caller-selected leaf is not a globally authoritative head.
   Historical activation does not establish present eligibility.
5. A structured binding can declare an exact parent, comparison, document view,
   source version, occurrence, context, region role and applicability scope/date.
   It preserves source identities and coordinate profiles rather than inferring
   legal role, effective time, or equivalence between different source views.

The positive effect is an authenticated declaration under explicitly supplied
trust premises. It does not verify human independence or competence. Original
unavailable contexts and deferred questions remain visible. None of these stages
promotes legal interpretation, training eligibility, semantic acceptance or Lean
proof/admission authority, and none trains or loads an autoencoder.

Completed declarations are separate records, explicitly bound to the reviewed

separate from meaning proposals. Population summaries preserve pending,

questions and unknown exposure. A generated bundle is not a completed review;
recorded declarations do not authenticate a reviewer, resolve context, adjudicate

prose is not automatically classified as agreement or disagreement.

supply native-view correspondence. It does not make its declarations compatible
with the separate V2 definition meaning-review interface. The imported five-owner
stack and V2 bridge keep their existing inputs and authority boundaries. The next
semantic step remains an actual independently reviewed definition/use component,
with explicit source/context bindings and a supported typed interpretation;
