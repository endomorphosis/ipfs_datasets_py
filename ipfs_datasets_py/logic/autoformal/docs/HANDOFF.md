## Concrete autoencoder improvement programme

1. **Freeze the task before changing architecture.** Choose complete reviewed typed content as the primary target. Keep source identity, reviewer/corpus authority and exact-text archival sidecars outside predicted semantic content. Target-derived vocabulary/role context is label information until an independently available inference route is declared. Distinguish vector reconstruction, compiler-guided conversion, learned AST generation and original-text restoration.
2. **Audit representability and complete target length.** Reuse typed IR, compact/counting codecs and normative-target preflights. Require lossless encode/decode for the actual complete norm, binders, ordered roles, modality, conditions, exceptions, temporal fields and cardinality. Reject an unsupported construct or inadequate output horizon explicitly. Do not drop a condition to fit the old 100-ID diagnostic budget or relabel an existing counted codec as the 537-token head.
3. **Create reviewed, exposure-aware groups.** Group repeated source occurrences, interpretation alternatives, definition families and dependency-connected components before splitting. Freeze train/development/test and permitted inference context; retain already exposed V3/R6/DEV cohorts as exposed. Start with a small feasibility slice, then acquire independent groups for quality promotion. A proposed 8-train/4-development/32-update-per-arm feasibility run is not an accuracy threshold or a held-out corpus claim.
4. **Select and restore a compatible retained parent.** Record complete producer/schema/codec/task/architecture/objective/tensor/optimizer lineage. Reuse existing profile inventory, ModelManager importer, checkpoint loaders and canonical experimental adapters. Later 384D/768D wording states declare `optimizer_resumable=false`: reuse their compatible weights and declare a fresh optimizer for further training. Context150's `tree_pin.py` and `ui_ux_ir/decoder.py` source deltas need explicit compatibility/migration before replay. Named-state/resume parity is a mechanics gate; it does not establish useful legal reconstruction. Do not pad/slice unrelated vectors, merge donor Adam moments or compare cross-space raw MSE.
5. **Resolve the next conditioning question once.** On 384D, compare the existing parameter-matched `first_step` and `every_step` paths with fixed data, objective, initialization, optimizer/update budget, decoder, selector and output budget. Keep the original initial-hidden-only donor as a separately labeled reference. A zero-start adapter proves wiring/parity only. The [existing retained-CP128 matched-reference plan](guides/legal_compact_content_role_warmstart_runtime_v1.md#matched-reference-coverage-plan-2026-10-23) specifies eight missing reference-score cells; these are not missing vectors in the completed four-width panel. Its separate reference API already has 23 authored contract cases/69 phases, while the genuine checkpoint matrices remain unrun. Fill only those cells through that owner after input/profile admission, not a new reconstruction campaign; this exposed authored diagnostic remains separate from the reviewed statutory-task experiment.
6. **Require source-dependence controls and autonomous output.** Use valid, zero and shuffled source conditioning with independently fixed legal context; include changed actor, ordered arguments, modality/negation, exception, definition scope and time. Score full BOS-to-EOS outputs over every enrolled source, including abstentions and failures. Reuse completed posthoc zero/source-sensitivity diagnostics as background; their shared exposed prefixes do not identify a universal causal forgetting mechanism.
7. **Change one intervention at a time.** Predicate weight 1→4/denominator 67→70, relation/binder heads, wider context, search/selector policy, bottleneck width and distillation each need their own comparison. Syntax-constrained search helps validity; forced constructors and teacher-forced likelihood are not autonomous semantic correctness. Preserve UNKNOWN/refusal and cumulative grammar/forward budgets.
8. **Expand the lane comparison after the frozen task works.** Evaluate the task-compatible fitted 768D parent, 8D controls and genuine 4096D lane on common eligible source groups and targets. Measure encoder families as a systems comparison. Isolate bottleneck effects in a separate experiment with source producer and task fixed. Extend context/output bounds only as explicit new profiles and measure costs before scaling.

Promotion requires a preregistered improvement in complete autonomous target fidelity and useful source-dependence on independent groups, with no severe scope/binding/exception/cardinality regressions and acceptable refusal/coverage. Choose numeric thresholds after the reviewed dataset and baseline are frozen; do not retrofit thresholds to exposed historical scores. If source controls are indistinguishable or complete exactness remains zero, inspect data, labels, information access, grammar capacity and failure classes before spending another fit budget.
