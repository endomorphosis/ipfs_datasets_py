# Source and context evidence review for the candidate corpus

`logic/autoformal/corpus_source_review.py` adds a bounded, complete review-request
bridge for the original parent and official-source evidence. It reuses the
parent/alignment adapters, context inventory, review handoff, dependency owner
and existing Ed25519 review verifier. It authenticates declarations under
explicitly selected trust inputs; it does not apply those declarations to corpus
contexts or qualify statutory interpretations.

## Complete source inputs and replay

`prepare_source_review_requests` accepts these keyword arguments:

- `alignment_corpus_bytes`: exactly `manifest`, `pages` and `records`, immutable
  bytes. The manifest is JSON; pages and records are complete JSONL with final
  newlines. `expected_alignment_corpus_pins` independently pins their raw SHA256s.
- `alignment_replay_inputs`: the complete keyword inputs of
  `build_parent_alignment_corpus`, excluding `limits`. This includes the original
  base-corpus transports, three original parent reports, nested original-parent
  replay, alignment report and every supplied official document. Do not pass a
  loader or validator callback.
- `source_report_bytes`: exactly `context` and `handoff`, holding complete
  original JSON reports. `expected_source_report_pins` supplies each report's
  `raw_sha256` and FULL `canonical_sha256`; these are distinct hash recipes.
- Optional `limits` lower named bounds. Raising or adding a bound is refused.

Raw hashes, immutable byte types, exact namespaces and nested byte budgets are
checked before operation-owner imports. The fixed owners regenerate the complete
alignment corpus and original context/handoff reports from the original source
inputs. Repaired hashes and self-seals cannot replace that replay. Importing the
public module itself performs no source decoding, crypto verification, network,
model, training or clock operation.

The preparation contains exactly `manifest`, `pages`, `requests`, `subjects` and
`dependency_manifests`. Every original queue unit is retained in its original
order. A request binds its original handoff pointer and whole value hash, its
original context pointer when present, exact parent/version/occurrence/context
CIDs, all supplied document comparisons and the complete relevant source-record
closure. Markup opportunities retain both source endpoints. Original unavailable
parent units remain explicit. No corpus context is changed.

Ordered pages bind request/subject CIDs and FULL hashes plus dependency manifest
hashes. Every record, page and manifest uses the unchanged common 1 MiB envelope.
The complete preparation has a separate 64 MiB cap, 8,192-request cap and
256-page cap, with at most 64 entries per page. Complete source inputs have a
128 MiB aggregate cap. Lower page limits repartition deterministically or refuse
rather than omit requests. Larger profiles require separate qualification.

`validate_source_review_requests(preparation, **original_inputs)` detaches and
bounds the complete offered preparation, repeats fixed-source replay once, and
compares every component against the regenerated result.

## Question scope and signing domain

Every request retains **16 distinct pending questions**: all six existing context
topics and all ten original handoff topics. Nine slots are active evidence
**declarations**: the six context topics and producer historical correspondence,
official edition authentication and region classification. Seven topics stay
explicitly deferred: definition, reference, exception and temporal closure,
IR semantic fidelity, global consistency and the Lean 4 kernel check.

The claim schema is `legal-corpus-source-context-review-signed-claim/v1`, action
`record_source_context_review_evidence/v1`, and audience
`legal-autoformalization-source-context-review/v1`. Prior graph and typed
native-definition claims/policies cannot authenticate in this domain. Key scope
IDs authorize the exact supplied parent-field review scope; they do not establish
governing legal applicability.

The shared verifier requires graph-shaped compatibility fields. Here `graph_id`
is the source request CID, `graph_sha256` is its FULL sealed-record hash and
`ast_sha256` is the complete sixteen-question hash. Each subject states these
recipes explicitly. No semantic graph or native AST is generated by this bridge.

Each request has its own compact dependency manifest, binding the full transport
hashes, question hash, relevant source records and producer source files. The
combined corpus is not flattened into one 4,096-artifact manifest. The existing
verifier supplies signature, role, principal, scope, time, revocation, evidence
and dependency checks unchanged. There is no public alternate verifier.

## Assess complete review groups

Call `assess_source_review_groups(preparation, registry, policy, review_groups,
**original_inputs, ...)` with independently selected preparation-root,
registry and policy SHA256s; `expected_review_group_pins`; and explicit
`at_time_utc`. There is no implicit current clock, signer, key registry or policy.

Provide one group for every request, in preparation order, including groups with
`reviews: []`. Each group contains exactly `request_cid`, `reviews`,
`evidence_artifacts` (immutable bytes by evidence ID),
`observed_artifact_sha256s` and `observed_pins`. Each independently selected pin
contains exactly `request_cid`, `reviews_sha256`, `evidence_sha256s`,
`observed_artifacts_sha256` and `observed_pins_sha256`.

The complete preparation, trust inputs, group pins and evidence groups are
bounded and detached before expensive source replay. Bodies must fit their
remaining group and batch budgets before hashing. The complete original source
preparation regenerates once for the batch, followed by one fixed-domain shared
assessment per request. Existing limits remain 32 reviews and 256 evidence
bodies per request, 64 registry keys and an 8 MiB per-assessment envelope; the
new whole-batch evidence cap is 64 MiB. Two required roles must have distinct
authorized principals. Different keys for one principal do not supply independence.

The result contains `manifest`, `pages` and `records`. Each group record retains
its nested shared-engine assessment and seven pending deferred slot IDs.
`validate_source_review_assessment(report, preparation, registry, policy,
review_groups, **assessment_inputs)` preflights the offered output's closed
schemas, seals and bounds before complete regeneration and comparison.

An unavailable-trust population with empty reviews yields pending groups.
Unknown, revoked or expired keys on submitted claims can yield rejected evidence.
Synthetic signatures
can test cryptographic behavior; externally shaped synthetic keys can test
selected-trust prerequisites. Neither proves human identity, independence,
competence, source truth or legal interpretation. Even authenticated declarations
leave source decisions unapplied, all original contexts required-unavailable and
all source/legal/semantic-graph/gold/Lean admission flags false. Actual reviewed
source decisions, semantic interpretation, scoped bindings and native lowering
remain separate work before qualified training or admitted semantic-corpus
publication. Candidate evidence may be retained or published with its explicit
unverified qualification state.
