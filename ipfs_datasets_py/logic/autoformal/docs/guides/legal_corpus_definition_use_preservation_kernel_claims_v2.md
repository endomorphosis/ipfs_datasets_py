# Closed kernel claims and planned theorem requests

The [retained elaboration reader](../../legal_corpus_definition_use_preservation_elaboration_packet_v2.py),
[kernel claim projection](../../legal_corpus_definition_use_preservation_kernel_claims_v2.py)
and [planned request owner](../../legal_corpus_definition_use_preservation_kernel_requests_v2.py)
connect retained Lean type observations to genuine `IRClaim`, `ProofObligation`
and `BackendRequest` values. These are engineering candidates and prospective
requests. Execution, typed theorem receipts and legal proof-cache admission
remain unqualified.

The original [opaque header lineage](legal_corpus_definition_use_preservation_theorem_lineage_v2.md)
is preserved. A new claim contains the complete closed Lean kernel proposition
and universe parameters as canonical JSON. It does not reinterpret the original
source header or rerun the [native elaboration profile](legal_corpus_definition_use_preservation_elaboration_v2.md).

## Replay complete retained evidence

`validate_definition_use_preservation_elaboration_packet_v2` accepts an immutable
sorted tuple of exactly 53 `(relative_path, bytes)` native files, the original
typed header lineage, the original 49-file preservation packet, complete corpus
preparation and assessment bytes. Its keyword arguments independently pin the
native receipt, standalone full-type payload, complete native bundle, complete
elaboration preparation and original source/review/packet inputs.

The reader reconstructs the public pure preparation before and after checking
the retained evidence. It checks all 17 frozen and executed source files, 13
nonempty compiled artifacts, logs, 15 complete original/alias type records,
their compact references and 30 empty axiom inventories per family. Historical
process lifecycle records are checked for internal consistency. Historical PIDs,
installed compilers and compiled artifacts are not executed or probed.

Complete current producer compatibility is mandatory. A changed producer cannot
fall back to a weaker historical profile. Supplied independent hashes remain
external provenance premises; an invented self-sealed success record does not
authenticate native execution. The original transport owns faithful byte and
filesystem identity acquisition. Installed standard and dynamic compiler
libraries remain external trust premises.

## Preserve the whole quantified proposition

`prepare_definition_use_preservation_kernel_claims_v2` requires the exact reader
observation pin and replays the reader on both sides of projection. It produces
15 genuine engineering claims per family. Each claim has one Lean4 obligation,
the full canonical kernel proposition statement, empty external assumptions
and empty obligation assumption IDs.

The captured types already quantify `hDonor`, `hConsumer` and `hAlias` where
those hypotheses appear. Their domains use de Bruijn references to preceding
type, interpretation and term binders. Every association therefore retains its
exact domain, binder position and complete preceding telescope context. These
open domains are never converted into independent closed facts.

Across the two families there are 68 expanded outer binders: 58 universal
parameters and ten proposition-premise occurrences. Earlier source-header
lineage reports 44 grouped universal parameter ranges, which expand into 58
universal kernel binders. The ten hypothesis binders are counted separately.
A group such as `{Entity Agent : Type}` expands into two kernel binders.
Quantified conditional theorems do not establish their hypotheses or prove a
statutory interpretation.

Stable claim identity binds original pure preparations and sources, assessment
and review state, the original interpretation and declared import-source
closure, type grammar/serializer, toolchain/binary commitments and external
library policy. Native receipts, PIDs, timings, logs, compiled artifact hashes,
generated alias identities and original header-claim digests remain separate
evidence. Changing only transient run observations must preserve semantic claim
identity while changing the corresponding evidence record. Declared source
imports do not establish the complete elaborated-term dependency closure.

## Prepare requests before qualifying execution

`prepare_definition_use_preservation_kernel_requests_v2` validates the complete
projection and creates one genuine `BackendRequest.for_claim` value for each
claim/obligation. `THEOREM_PROOF` is requested. The proposed backend is
`legal-corpus-v2-closed-kernel-theorem-native@1`, with encoding
`lean4-kernel-proposition-json/v1`. This profile has no qualified execution
adapter. Its `dispatch_qualified` and `execution_profile_qualified` fields are
false.

Request identity binds the exact claim/obligation, full kernel statement,
projection and evidence commitments, finite requested bounds, backend/encoding
and resource policy. Public validation regenerates all typed requests and
compares their complete type-sensitive storage. Exact registered nested classes
are checked before conversion; inherited producer pins cannot be overwritten;
argument trees are bounded before hashing. Route and policy constants are fixed.

Finite `ExecutionBounds` describe requested limits. They are not measurements or
evidence that the limits were enforced. The prospective policy requires observed
elapsed time, steps, peak memory and complete output accounting before an
affirmative theorem result or `ProofReceipt`:

| Metric | Remaining qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Elapsed milliseconds | Whole invocation including bounded cleanup; never clamp observed time to the requested limit |
| Steps | Explicit units and enforcement over the chosen request scope; Lean `maxHeartbeats` is per command and uses thousands of internal allocation heartbeats |
| Peak memory bytes | Qualified measurement and aggregate scope; sampled RSS and per-process limits do not establish an exact aggregate peak |
| Output bytes | Complete accounting of captured logs, returned artifacts, full types and result payload under an explicit accounting policy |

Missing telemetry cannot become an observed zero. A future adapter must create
the request before fresh execution, bind its digest into native observations,
and produce genuine attempt/result/output bindings. Existing elaboration
receipts contain no request digest and cannot be converted retrospectively into
request-scoped proof receipts. Shared invocation mappings require separate
qualification.

This step does not establish statutory correspondence, legal admission,
autoencoder improvement, full task satisfaction or model convergence. Those
require reviewed source meanings, exact legal artifact/obligation/receipt
binding, dependency invalidation, coherent corpus release and held-out model
evaluation after genuine execution qualification.
