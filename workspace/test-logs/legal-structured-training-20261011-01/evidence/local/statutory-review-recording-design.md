## A distinct contract

Use a new closed schema, `statutory-source-context-review-submission/v1`, rather than editing a blank packet or relabeling an authored review. Each submission contains its own declared submission ID, the externally selected full-packet canonical SHA, and a bounded subset of review items. Bind every item to its exact `item_id`, `input_sha256`, `physical_identity_sha256`, selected source-text SHA, complete context artifact SHA/size and selected context character offsets. The parent packet validates all of these joins, raw-byte selectors and archived physical provenance before recording; no submitted envelope can change them.

Keep canonical full-content SHA, embedded body-content SHA and exact file-byte SHA distinct. ## Annotation boundaries

Retain the existing `review` and `context_review` questions, with null meaning unanswered and explicit empty values meaning a declared absence. Type the fields in the new submission profile: normative eligibility; definition and multiple-rule dispositions; interpretation status; ordered normative rules; selected-source literal role anchors; qualifier attachments and scope; trigger evidence; ambiguity/unsupported-meaning findings; complete-section sufficiency; operative/editorial boundaries; definitions, references and unresolved dependencies. Require declared reviewer ID and a valid UTC calendar timestamp for a declaration to be structurally complete. Completeness remains a slot-filling fact.

Anchor records should state selected-source Unicode character coordinates, half-open offsets, literal text, rule index and role. Verify exact source slices and valid rule/anchor references. Context anchors must use a different explicit coordinate space and a context-artifact join. Store multi-rule, repeated-surface, overlapping and multiple-qualifier interpretations without flattening them into the current one-rule decoder profile. Qualifier scope should identify the affected rule indices and retain explanatory wording. Explicit non-copyable or context-derived meaning must remain a declared coverage gap, never an invented source anchor.

Compute a separate exact declaration signature from interpretation-bearing fields, including context findings, eligibility, ordered rules, role anchors, attachments and scope. Exclude reviewer ID/time, free notes and evidence references from mechanical agreement. Do not normalize rule order, compare only copied strings, infer paraphrase equivalence or resolve disagreement from model outputs. Report pending, partial, single complete declaration, exact multiple-declaration agreement, disputed, ambiguous and unsupported dispositions. Two different declared IDs are not two authenticated independent people.

## Evidence, adjudication and admission

Submission evidence references may bind exact bytes for declared identity, independence, exposure and review-process documents. Check their externally selected SHA/size and opening/closing bytes; report integrity separately from authentication. Do not let a submission select a verifier, registry or policy. An ID, timestamp, explanatory document or valid signature alone does not authenticate a human, independence, legal meaning, currentness or training rights. Record known candidate/reference exposure; do not derive blindness or a fresh holdout from omission.

A later, separate evidence package may record an externally declared adjudicator and decision over exact submission/declaration hashes, considered interpretations, rationale, unresolved scope and process/evidence references. Validate reference coverage and decision cardinality. Keep `external_adjudication_status` and actual independent completion pending, even when an adjudication declaration is internally consistent. A selected admission owner must independently verify process identity, independence, source authority, semantic coverage, supported target/family derivation, exposure-group policy and applicable fit/evaluation rights before producing an admitted target. This recorder never does so.

Every output must retain zero `weak_decoder_fit`, `strong_semantic_fit`, `contrastive_supervision`, `proof_supervision` and `fidelity_evaluation` masks. All semantic-gold, authority, independent-review, family/Lake and actual-admission flags remain false. No model, provider, encoder, compiler, prover or training call belongs in this workflow. Geometry can be attached as a distinct diagnostic after an explicit caller-declared proposal; successful geometry must not alter a declaration, create a target or promote masks.
