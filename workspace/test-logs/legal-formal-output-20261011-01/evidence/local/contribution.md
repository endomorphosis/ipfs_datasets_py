declarations, the existing native deontic parser, regenerated Lean and the
existing bounded `lake build legal` gate. The adapter preserves complete reports
and canonical IR, replays scored raw/joint selections, and refuses unsupported

drafts remain in evidence. No new model forwards or optimizer updates occurred.

this profile. Temporal, TDFOL, DCEC, FOL and name-alias routes remain explicit
